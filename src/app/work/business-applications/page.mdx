## Overview

For more than a decade, I have built order management systems, billing automation, field operations tools, scheduling systems, and the reporting that ties them together. I bring that experience to each Pineflux application.

A business often starts with a spreadsheet because it is flexible and everyone can use it. Trouble starts when five people edit the same file, nobody trusts the totals, and a formula or missed update can affect an order. At that point, the workflow needs a better home.

A custom application puts the workflow, data, and business rules in one place. Your team enters information once. Approvals follow the right path. Everyone sees the current status of an order, job, or customer without asking around.

We begin with the workflow causing the most trouble. That keeps the first release focused and lets you judge the value before adding more.
