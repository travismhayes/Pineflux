## Overview

I have spent over a decade building business applications professionally: order management systems, billing and invoicing automation, field operations tools, scheduling systems, and the reporting layers that sit on top of them. That is the experience every Pineflux project gets.

The pattern is almost always the same. A business starts with spreadsheets because they are free and flexible. A few years later, the spreadsheet has become the business: five people edit it, nobody trusts it, and one formula typo can cost real money. The work has outgrown the tool.

A custom business application fixes that by matching the software to the workflow instead of forcing the workflow to match the software. Your team enters data once. Everyone sees the same numbers. The rules of your business, who approves what, what happens when an order lands, which customers get which pricing, live in the system instead of in one employee's head.

Projects start small on purpose. We identify the single most painful workflow, build the tool that fixes it, and expand from there once it is earning its keep.
