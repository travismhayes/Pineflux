## Overview

A business website should turn interest into a useful next step. Design, copy, page speed, and search structure all contribute to that job.

Pages need to load quickly on phones and weak connections. Search engines need a clear explanation of what you offer and where you work. Visitors need enough detail to decide whether you fit, followed by an obvious way to call, book, or send an inquiry.

You will be able to edit routine content yourself. I choose an editing setup that matches how often the site changes, while keeping the pages lean and fast.

For paid campaigns, I can build focused landing pages around a specific audience and offer, with conversion tracking in place from launch.
