## Overview

A business website has one job: turn the right visitors into conversations. Everything else, the design, the copy, the speed, the search ranking, either serves that job or gets out of the way.

The technical fundamentals are non-negotiable and often neglected. Pages load fast, on phones and on weak connections, because slow sites lose visitors before the headline renders. The structure is built so search engines can understand what you do and where you do it. And every page gives the visitor one obvious next step: call, book, or send an inquiry.

Just as important is what I will not do. You will not get a bloated page builder that takes eight seconds to load, a template with your logo swapped in, or a maintenance hostage situation where small text changes require a developer. Sites are built so you can edit your own content.

For businesses running paid ads or campaigns, focused landing pages carry the same discipline: one audience, one message, one action, and measurement in place so you know what the campaign actually produced.
