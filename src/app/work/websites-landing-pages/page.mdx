## Overview

A business website has one job: turning the right visitors into conversations. Every decision about design, copy, and speed gets measured against that.

The technical fundamentals get skipped surprisingly often, so they get extra attention here. Pages load fast, on phones and on weak connections, because slow sites lose visitors before the headline renders. The structure is built so search engines can understand what you do and where you do it. And every page gives the visitor one obvious next step: call, book, or send an inquiry.

You will be able to edit your own content. Small text changes should never require paying a developer, and you will not get a bloated page builder that takes eight seconds to load.

For businesses running paid ads or campaigns, focused landing pages carry the same discipline: one audience, one message, one action, and measurement in place so you know what the campaign actually produced.
