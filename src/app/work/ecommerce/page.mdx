## Overview

The difficult part of ecommerce often begins after checkout. Inventory needs to update, the warehouse needs the right order, taxes and payments need to reconcile, and returns need a clear path. A good-looking storefront only solves the front of that process.

Much of my career has been spent where storefronts meet ERPs, accounting systems, and warehouses. I design the customer-facing store and the back-office flow as parts of the same system.

On the storefront, that means fast pages, clear product information, and fewer distractions in checkout. Behind it, orders and inventory move between systems with less manual handling.

Pineflux can build a new store, improve an existing one, or add functionality your platform does not provide. The work starts with how you sell and fulfill today, then fixes the part creating the most friction.
