## Overview

Getting systems to talk to each other is the core of what I have done professionally for the last decade: ERP integrations, API development, data synchronization between platforms that were never designed to cooperate, and automation of the workflows in between.

The symptoms are easy to recognize. An order comes in through the website and someone re-types it into the ERP. Inventory lives in two places and matches in neither. Month-end reporting means exporting three CSVs and stitching them together by hand. Every one of those handoffs costs time and introduces errors.

The fix is rarely glamorous, and that is the point. A well-built integration is invisible: the order that lands in your storefront simply appears in your ERP, the invoice simply posts to your accounting system, and the stock count is simply right. Your team gets hours back every week, and the numbers you make decisions with become numbers you can trust.

I work with the systems you already have. The goal is never to sell you a migration you do not need; it is to make what you own work together.

## What this looks like in practice

Integrations I have personally built include ERP-to-field-operations syncs that eliminated duplicate order entry, billing pipelines that connected usage data to invoicing automatically, and API layers that let legacy systems serve modern web and mobile front ends.

Every integration ships with logging, monitoring, and alerting, because an integration you cannot see into is a liability, not an asset.
